## DUE DATE

Projects for all groups are due no later than **end of day November 15, 2026.**

## General instructions

- AI/LLM usage is allowed for coding tasks but must be explicitly declared, including which tools were used.
  - AI/LLM usage is not permitted for the project report (i.e the questions where students shall explain their methodology, reasoning, and/or work). AI usage for these tasks will incur a -X penalty per question/incident. 
- Project must be on GitHub.
  - Files must be committed normally. Files submitted by upload or not allowed and will (not be graded/incur a penalty).
  - Each question/point of the project shall be a different mr. All mrs must be reviewed by another member of the group. 
  - Each member of the group is expected to have roughly similar activity on the project ( assessed by both number/quality of the commits and activity in mrs)
- Generally, late projects will not be accepted. Those that are accepted must be approved by the instructor PRIOR to the initial due date and will incur a -1 point penalty for each day late beyond the initial due date.
